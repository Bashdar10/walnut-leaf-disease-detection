Treatment and Management Recommendations

Use these recommendations as supportive guidance and consult agricultural specialists before applying treatments.

Best Practices for Image Capture

  • Upload clear images.
  • Use good lighting.
  • Capture the upper leaf surface.
  • Avoid blurry images.
  • Use one leaf as the main subject whenever possible.

Orchard Management Practices

  • Monitor orchards regularly.
  • Remove heavily infected leaves.
  • Improve sanitation and reduce plant stress.
  • Consult agricultural specialists before using treatments.
  • Do not consider any result as guaranteed agricultural certainty.
